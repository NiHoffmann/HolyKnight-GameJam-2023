Enemies explained:

Skeletons: Can't be killed only weakness is light

Blackflyers: Can be stunned by attacking (light disappears)

    Are vulnerable to bright light (statue) -> Black particles show invulnerability

    Red/Blue light shows if a bird is attacking (Red is attacking)

Brownflyers: Disable Street lights

     can be stunned (for a couple of seconds) by attacking

Streetlight: will reenable if brown-flyers weren't close for a set period of time

Boss-Fight: Reflect orbs by timing an attack

    The Evil mushroom king utilizes Shadowenergie and turns the world dark

    Weaken the Mushroom king to restore light to the world

    spawns minions to support him in his fight

Keys: can be dropped by killing skeletons
